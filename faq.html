    What is the Service Navigation Assistant?

    It is a simple website tool designed to help visitors find service information and answers to common questions. It provides navigation, not professional advice.

    Does the assistant provide crisis counselling?

    No. It does not assess or provide crisis counselling. If a message suggests immediate danger or a safety concern, the prototype displays a human-support hand-off notice.

    What services can I explore?

    The prototype includes accommodation, social work, psychological and wellbeing support, NDIS navigation, chaplaincy, and jobs or employment pathways.

    Do I need to know which service I need?

    No. You can browse the service descriptions or use the assistant to explore a topic. A staff member can help clarify options.

    Is the information guaranteed to be current?

    No. prototype. Confirm service availability, eligibility, opening hours, and referral requirements directly with the organisation.

    Does this prototype save my personal information?

    The front-end prototype does not send messages to a server or intentionally save them. Avoid entering personal, identifying, or sensitive information into demonstration websites.

    What should I do if I am in immediate danger?

    Do not rely on this website or the assistant. Contact local emergency services or a verified crisis support service immediately.
