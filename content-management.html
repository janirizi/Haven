    STAFF CONTENT AREA

    How approved content is managed.

    Service information on this website changes only after it has been checked and approved. This page explains each step and who is responsible for it.

    THE APPROVAL PROCESS

    Five steps before anything goes live

    Every change to service information, contact details or safety wording follows the same path.

    1. Request a change

    Any staff member can request a change by describing it and naming the page or service it affects. Never include client or personal details.

    2. Draft

    The content editor prepares the new wording using only approved service information.

    3. Check

    The content owner checks accuracy, eligibility and contact details against official Haven House records.

    4. Approve

    The approver signs off the final wording. Safety-related wording also needs the safety reviewer's sign-off.

    5. Publish and record

    The publisher updates the website, checks links, and records the date and approver in the change log.

    ASSIGNED STAFF

    Who looks after what

    Each role has a named staff member and a backup. Names are set in js/staff-roles.js and should be updated whenever responsibilities change.

    STANDING RULES

    Keeping content current

    Review every six months

    The content owner re-checks all service pages, even when nothing has changed.

    Contact details first

    Address, mobile and email are updated as soon as they change, and checked before the next publish.

    Keep a change log

    Record what changed, when, who drafted it and who approved it, so every version can be traced.
